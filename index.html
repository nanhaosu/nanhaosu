HUST Trustworthy AI Research Group · May 2026–Present

Agent memory reliability & evaluation

Undergraduate research on the behavior of memory-enabled language-model agents.

  • Investigated A-MEM and a fixed-version Mem0 implementation through controlled synthetic-data experiments.
  • Studied long-term memory updates, state consistency, and downstream retrieval behavior using test traces and controlled comparisons.
  • Documented 54 A-MEM experimental configurations and 72 matched replay units in internal research records; developed preliminary evaluation protocols and pilot studies.
Ongoing research · technical details available for discussion as appropriate.
Provincial Undergraduate Innovation Project · 2026–Present

LLM-based telecom-fraud data augmentation

Literature-informed synthetic dialogue generation for fraud-related model research.

  • Adapted augmentation ideas from TeleAntiFraud-28k (ACM MM 2025) and Bot Wars Evolved (2025).
  • Used Qwen and DeepSeek with an existing corpus of 66,699 fraud case descriptions; contributed to generating 10,000 annotated synthetic dialogues.
  • Contributed to prompt refinement, quality assessment, and evaluation workflows for lightweight fraud detection.
Team project · literature-informed augmentation and data quality work.
